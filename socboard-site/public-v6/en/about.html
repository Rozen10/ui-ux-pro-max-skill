About

Big-company security, at your scale.

SOCBoard grew out of a simple observation: most businesses hold sensitive data without the protection that large groups can afford. We designed a solution to close that gap.

The solution

A platform that measures. A team that validates.

SOCBoard combines an analysis platform, which examines your company’s exposure and configuration every month, with a team of cybersecurity experts who check, prioritise and explain every finding.

Our analysts come from incident detection and response. They know how attacks are prepared and what they exploit first.

Our method is based on the ISO 27001 standard: verifiable findings, justified priorities, tracking over time.

The method

The platform measures. Our analysts judge.

  1. MeasureThe SOCBoard platform examines your exposure and configuration, the same way every month.
  2. CheckEvery finding is checked by our analysts. False positives are removed before they reach your report.
  3. PrioritiseRisks are ranked by their impact on a business: confidentiality, client data, continuity, reputation.
  4. ExplainThe report is written for management. Technical details are provided separately for your IT provider.
