Frequently asked questions

Security, with no grey areas.

The key answers to know whether SOCBoard is right for your company.

All questions →
Do we need an in-house IT team?

No. SOCBoard is designed for companies that rely on an IT provider and have no dedicated security lead. Findings and actions are written to be understood by management and by your provider alike.

Is it a solution for experts only?

No. The whole point is to make security accessible. Every risk is presented with its impact and a recommended action, with no prior technical knowledge needed.

How does the Diagnostic work?

We define the scope with you, examine what matters in your context, then present an assessment and a prioritised action plan in a clear debrief. Its price, €690 excl. VAT, is deductible from your first month’s subscription.

Does SOCBoard replace our security tools?

No. SOCBoard helps you understand and steer your security based on your existing tools and practices. It complements your antivirus, your backups and your IT provider, without replacing them.

Do you monitor the company continuously?

No. SOCBoard is a monthly expert analysis, not 24/7 monitoring or real-time alerting. If an incident is under way, contact your IT provider immediately.
