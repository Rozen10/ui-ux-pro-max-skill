Who it’s for

For every business that wants to see clearly.

SOCBoard is for businesses that handle important data, whether clients, finances or know-how, without an in-house security team.

What’s at stake

Very real risks. Rarely visible.

  • Payment fraud and impersonation. If your email address can be spoofed, a fraudster can write to your clients or suppliers in your name. The report checks this protection every month.
  • Exposed credentials. An employee password found in a leak can be enough to open the mailbox. We look for them for you.
  • Business continuity. Downtime at the wrong moment is costly. Backups and remote access are among the priorities we examine.
  • Questions from clients and insurers. A documented history of your security level makes answering them easier.
