How it works

Examine, explain, follow up.

The process is simple and predictable. At every step you know what we are doing, what we expect from you and what you receive.

  1. 120 minutes

    A first call

    A call with our team to understand your company: size, organisation, IT provider, client requirements. We tell you frankly whether SOCBoard is a good fit.

    You receive

    • A written Diagnostic proposal
    • A confidentiality agreement
  2. 2Weeks 1 and 2

    The Diagnostic

    An interview with management, then a review of your environment: email and domain name, remote access, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace accounts, credentials exposed in public leaks, backup practices.

    We work with your IT provider, without disrupting the company’s activity.

    We need

    • One hour with management
    • A contact at your IT provider
    • Read-only access, if you choose to grant it
  3. 3Week 3

    The debrief

    Our analysts present the baseline report to you by video: your health index, your risk level, the weak points explained in terms of their consequences for the business, and a roadmap ranked by priority.

    You receive

    • The baseline report, validated and signed
    • A 90-day roadmap
    • A one-page summary for management
  4. 4Every month

    Monthly follow-up

    With Radar or Shield, the review is repeated every month and compared with the previous one. You see what has improved, what has appeared, and the three priorities for the month. With Shield, a 30-minute video call lets you discuss it directly with your SOCBoard analyst.

    You receive

    • A monthly report validated by our analysts
    • The history of your health index
    • Answers to your questions, without jargon

Discretion

Your client files stay with you.

We analyse your company’s configuration and exposure, not the content of your files. We need no access to your clients’ documents.

Any access is read-only, time-limited and withdrawn at your request. Reports are only shared with the people you designate.

A confidentiality agreement is signed before any information is exchanged.

The limits, stated clearly

A monthly rhythm, not an alarm.

SOCBoard is an expert analysis solution with a monthly rhythm. It does not monitor your network in real time and does not trigger an immediate alert if an incident occurs.

Its role is to reduce, month after month, the likelihood of an incident, and to give you a clear, documented view of your security level.

If you think you are under attack right now, contact your IT provider without delay and, in France, the cybermalveillance.gouv.fr service.
