The solution

Does SOCBoard monitor my company 24/7?

No. SOCBoard is an expert analysis solution with a monthly rhythm. It does not monitor your network in real time and does not trigger an immediate alert if an incident occurs. Its role is to reduce, month after month, the risk of an incident happening.

What happens if I am attacked?

Contact your IT provider immediately. In France, the cybermalveillance.gouv.fr service directs you to assistance professionals. SOCBoard is not an emergency response solution.

How is it different from an antivirus or security software?

Software protects a workstation or a server. SOCBoard gives you an expert opinion on the whole picture: what is exposed, what is misconfigured, what should be handled first. It complements your tools; it does not replace them.

Who writes and validates the reports?

Every report is checked, written and signed by the analysts of the SOCBoard team, following a method based on the ISO 27001 standard. The platform measures; the conclusions are always human.

Confidentiality and access

Do you have access to my clients’ files?

No. We analyse your company’s configuration and exposure, never the content of your files. The confidentiality of your exchanges is never put at stake.

What access do you need?

Much of the analysis is done from the outside, with no access at all. To go further (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace), we ask for read-only access, which you can withdraw at any time.

Do you sign a confidentiality agreement?

Yes, before any information about your company is exchanged.

Should we tell our IT provider?

We recommend it. SOCBoard works with them, not against them: our priorities give them a clear roadmap, and they remain in charge of any work.

Pricing and commitment

Is the Diagnostic mandatory?

It is required before Shield and recommended in every case: it sets the starting point that each monthly report is compared against. Its price, €690 excl. VAT at launch, is deducted from your first month’s subscription.

What is the difference between Radar and Shield?

Radar (€299 excl. VAT/month, or €239 with an annual commitment) analyses your external exposure every month and gives you a validated report. Shield (€549 excl. VAT/month, or €449 with an annual commitment) adds a review of your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace environment and a monthly video call with your analyst.
